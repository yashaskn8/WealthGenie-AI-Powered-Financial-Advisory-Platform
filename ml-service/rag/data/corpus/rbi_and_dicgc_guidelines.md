# RBI & DICGC Regulatory Guidelines on Deposits, Insurance & Sovereign Securities

*Official Sources:* Reserve Bank of India (RBI) (`https://www.rbi.org.in`) & Deposit Insurance and Credit Guarantee Corporation (DICGC) (`https://www.dicgc.org.in`)  
*Regulatory Framework:* DICGC Act, 1961 (as amended) & RBI Master Directions on Retail Savings Instruments  
*Trust Tier:* Tier 1 - Official Government / Central Bank Regulatory Bodies  

---

## 1. DICGC Deposit Insurance Coverage Framework

The Deposit Insurance and Credit Guarantee Corporation (DICGC), a wholly owned subsidiary of the Reserve Bank of India (RBI), provides statutory deposit insurance to protect retail depositors in Indian banks.

### 1.1 Insurance Coverage Limit
- **Maximum Protection Limit**: Each depositor in an insured bank is insured up to a maximum limit of **₹5,00,000 (Five Lakh Rupees)** for both principal and accrued interest combined.
- **Scope of Insured Institutions**: Covers all commercial banks (including foreign bank branches in India, Regional Rural Banks [RRBs], Local Area Banks, and Small Finance Banks) and all primary cooperative banks (urban, state, and central cooperative banks).

### 1.2 Capacity and Right Rules (Same Right vs Different Capacity)
- **Aggregation Rule**: All deposit accounts (savings, fixed deposit [FD], current account, recurring deposit [RD]) held by a depositor in the **same branch or different branches of the same bank** in the *same right and same capacity* are added together for the ₹5 Lakh limit.
- **Separate Capacity Protections**: If deposits are held in different rights or capacities, each capacity qualifies for a separate insurance cover up to ₹5,00,000 per bank:
  1. Individual Account (in single name).
  2. Joint Account (held jointly; accounts with different order of names e.g. A & B vs B & A are treated as distinct capacities).
  3. Partner of a Partnership Firm.
  4. Sole Proprietorship Account.
  5. Guardian of a Minor Account.
  6. Trustee of a Trust.

### 1.3 Exclusions from DICGC Coverage
The following deposits and financial instruments are **explicitly excluded** from DICGC deposit insurance:
- Deposits of foreign Governments.
- Deposits of Central and State Governments.
- Inter-bank deposits (deposits held by one bank in another bank).
- Deposits in Non-Banking Financial Companies (NBFCs).
- Mutual fund units, equity stocks, corporate bonds, commercial paper, and debentures.
- Government securities, RBI Floating Rate Savings Bonds, and Sovereign Gold Bonds.

## 2. RBI Floating Rate Savings Bonds, 2020 (Taxable)

RBI Floating Rate Savings Bonds (FRSB 2020) are sovereign debt instruments issued by the Reserve Bank of India on behalf of the Government of India.

### 2.1 Key Instrument Features
- **Issuer**: Government of India (sovereign guarantee; zero credit default risk).
- **Tenure**: Fixed tenure of **7 years** from the date of issue.
- **Interest Rate Structure**: Floating interest rate linked to the prevailing rate on National Savings Certificates (NSC).
  - The interest rate is set at **NSC Rate + 0.35% (35 basis points)**.
  - Interest is reset semi-annually on **January 1** and **July 1** every year.
- **Payout Frequency**: Interest is payable semi-annually on January 1 and July 1. No cumulative option is available.
- **Taxability**: Interest earned is **fully taxable** at the investor's applicable income tax slab rate. Tax Deducted at Source (TDS) applies as per Income Tax Act provisions.
- **Transferability & Liquidity**: Non-transferable and non-tradable in secondary markets. Premature redemption is permitted only for senior citizens (aged 60 and above) after specified minimum lock-in periods (4 to 6 years depending on age bracket).

## 3. Sovereign Gold Bonds (SGB) Framework

Sovereign Gold Bonds are government securities denominated in grams of gold issued by the RBI on behalf of the Government of India as an alternative to holding physical gold.

### 3.1 Key Features & Tax Treatment
- **Tenure**: 8 years, with an exit option available after 5 years (exercisable on interest payment dates).
- **Assured Interest**: Fixed interest rate of **2.50% per annum** payable semi-annually on the nominal initial investment value.
- **Taxation on Interest**: Annual interest income of 2.5% is taxable at the investor's applicable tax slab rate.
- **Capital Gains Tax Exemption**: Capital gains arising from redemption of SGB at maturity (after 8 years) by an individual are **100% exempt from Long-Term Capital Gains (LTCG) tax** under Section 47(viib) of the Income Tax Act. Indexation benefit is available if transferred prior to maturity.
