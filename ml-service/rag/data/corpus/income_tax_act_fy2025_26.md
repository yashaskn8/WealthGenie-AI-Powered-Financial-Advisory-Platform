# Income Tax Department Regulations & Provisions (FY 2025-26 / AY 2026-27)

*Official Source:* Central Board of Direct Taxes (CBDT), Ministry of Finance, Government of India (`https://www.incometaxindia.gov.in`)  
*Effective Assessment Year:* AY 2026-27 (Financial Year 2025-26)  
*Trust Tier:* Tier 1 - Official Government Body  

---

## 1. New Tax Regime (Section 115BAC — Default Regime)

Under Section 115BAC of the Income Tax Act, 1961, as updated for Financial Year 2025-26, the New Tax Regime is the default tax regime for all individual taxpayers.

### 1.1 New Tax Slabs & Marginal Rates
- **Up to ₹4,00,000**: NIL (0%)
- **₹4,00,001 to ₹8,00,000**: 5%
- **₹8,00,001 to ₹12,00,000**: 10%
- **₹12,00,001 to ₹16,00,000**: 15%
- **₹16,00,001 to ₹20,00,000**: 20%
- **₹20,00,001 to ₹24,00,000**: 25%
- **Above ₹24,00,000**: 30%

### 1.2 Standard Deduction & Section 87A Rebate
- **Standard Deduction**: Salaried individuals and pensioners are entitled to a flat standard deduction of **₹75,000** under the New Tax Regime.
- **Section 87A Tax Rebate**: Resident individuals with total taxable income up to **₹12,00,000** receive a full tax rebate under Section 87A, resulting in zero net tax liability.
- **Marginal Relief u/s 87A**: Taxpayers whose taxable income marginally exceeds ₹12,00,000 receive marginal relief so that the tax payable does not exceed the income earned above ₹12,00,000.

## 2. Old Tax Regime (Opt-Out Option)

Taxpayers may explicitly choose to opt out of the New Tax Regime and be taxed under the Old Tax Regime when filing their Income Tax Return (ITR).

### 2.1 Old Tax Slabs
- **Up to ₹2,50,000**: NIL (0%)
- **₹2,50,001 to ₹5,00,000**: 5%
- **₹5,00,001 to ₹10,00,000**: 20%
- **Above ₹10,00,000**: 30%
- Standard Deduction under Old Regime: **₹50,000** for salaried individuals.

### 2.2 Deductions and Exemptions under Old Regime
- **Section 80C**: Deduction up to a maximum ceiling of **₹1,50,000** per financial year for eligible investments:
  - Equity Linked Savings Scheme (ELSS) mutual funds (with mandatory 3-year lock-in).
  - Public Provident Fund (PPF) and Employees Provident Fund (EPF).
  - Principal repayment on home loans.
  - National Savings Certificates (NSC), Sukanya Samriddhi Yojana (SSY), and 5-year tax-saver FDs.
- **Section 80D (Health Insurance)**:
  - Self, Spouse, and Dependent Children: Maximum **₹25,000** (or **₹50,000** if senior citizen aged 60+).
  - Parents: Additional deduction up to **₹25,000** (or **₹50,000** if parents are senior citizens).
  - Preventive health check-up sub-limit: Up to **₹5,000** within the overall 80D limit.
- **Section 80CCD(1B) (National Pension System - NPS)**:
  - Additional deduction of up to **₹50,000** over and above the ₹1,50,000 limit of Section 80C for voluntary individual contributions to NPS (Tier-I account).
- **Section 10(13A) (House Rent Allowance - HRA)**:
  - Exemption calculated as the minimum of:
    1. Actual HRA received from employer.
    2. Rent paid minus 10% of basic salary.
    3. 50% of basic salary (for metro cities: Delhi, Mumbai, Kolkata, Chennai) or 40% (non-metro).

## 3. Capital Gains Tax Provisions (Finance Act Framework)

Capital gains taxation rules vary depending on asset classification, holding duration, and Securities Transaction Tax (STT) payment status.

### 3.1 Listed Equity Shares & Equity Mutual Funds (STT Paid)
- **Short-Term Capital Gains (STCG)** (Holding period <= 12 months): Taxed at **20%**.
- **Long-Term Capital Gains (LTCG)** (Holding period > 12 months): Taxed at **12.5%** on aggregate gains exceeding **₹1,25,000** per financial year (gains up to ₹1.25 Lakh are fully exempt).

### 3.2 Debt Mutual Funds & Specified Mutual Funds
- Debt mutual fund investments made on or after April 1, 2023 (where equity investment in Indian companies is <= 35%) are treated as short-term capital assets regardless of holding period and gains are taxed at the taxpayer's applicable income tax slab rate.

### 3.3 Real Estate, Gold, and Unlisted Securities
- **Holding Period**: Assets held for > 24 months qualify as Long-Term Capital Assets.
- **LTCG Rate**: Taxed at **12.5%** without indexation benefits.
- **Pre-July 23, 2024 Grandfathering for Real Estate**: For land or buildings acquired before July 23, 2024, individual resident taxpayers may opt to pay tax at either 12.5% without indexation or 20% with indexation, whichever yields lower tax.
