# Master Reference on Income Tax Deductions, Rebates & Allowances (FY 2025-26)

*Official Source:* Central Board of Direct Taxes (CBDT), Government of India (`https://www.incometaxindia.gov.in`)  
*Trust Tier:* Tier 1 - Official Government Body

## 1. Statutory Exemption & Rebate Master Schedules

### 1.1 Section 87A Tax Rebate Detailed Eligibility
- **New Tax Regime Threshold**: Net taxable income up to ₹12,00,000 qualifies for maximum rebate of ₹60,000.
- **Marginal Relief Formula**: If Net Income (NI) > ₹12,00,000, Tax Payable = min(Computed Tax, NI - ₹12,00,000).
- **Old Tax Regime Threshold**: Net taxable income up to ₹5,00,000 qualifies for maximum rebate of ₹12,500.

### 1.2 Section 80TTA and Section 80TTB Interest Deductions
- **Section 80TTA (Savings Account Interest)**: Individual taxpayers (aged < 60 years) can claim deduction up to ₹10,000 per financial year for interest earned on savings accounts with commercial banks, cooperative banks, or post offices under the Old Tax Regime.
- **Section 80TTB (Senior Citizens Interest Income)**: Senior citizen resident taxpayers (aged 60 years and above) can claim deduction up to ₹50,000 per financial year for interest earned on savings accounts, fixed deposits (FD), and recurring deposits (RD) under the Old Tax Regime.

### 1.3 Section 80GG — Deduction for Rent Paid When HRA is Not Received
- Taxpayers who do not receive House Rent Allowance (HRA) from employer can claim deduction under Section 80GG for rent paid for residential accommodation.
- Maximum deduction is the least of:
  1. ₹5,000 per month (₹60,000 per year).
  2. 25% of Total Income.
  3. Actual Rent Paid minus 10% of Total Income.
