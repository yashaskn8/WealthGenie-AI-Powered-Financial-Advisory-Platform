# SEBI Mutual Fund Scheme Categorization, Allocation Rules & Risk-o-meter Guidelines

*Official Source:* Securities and Exchange Board of India (SEBI) & Association of Mutual Funds in India (AMFI) (`https://www.sebi.gov.in`)  
*Regulatory Framework:* SEBI Master Circular for Mutual Funds (SEBI/HO/IMD/DF3/CIR/P/2017/114 as amended in 2024-2026)  
*Trust Tier:* Tier 1 - Official Regulatory Body  

---

## SECTION 1: SEBI MUTUAL FUND CATEGORIZATION FRAMEWORK

To standardize scheme definitions, ensure transparency, and protect retail investors from misleading scheme names, SEBI mandates that Asset Management Companies (AMCs) categorize mutual fund schemes into five main groups: Equity Schemes, Debt Schemes, Hybrid Schemes, Solution Oriented Schemes, and Other Schemes.

### 1.1 Detailed Equity Scheme Definitions & Allocation Mandates

1. **Multi Cap Fund**:
   - **Investment Objective**: Open-ended equity scheme investing across Large Cap, Mid Cap, and Small Cap stocks.
   - **Asset Allocation Mandate**: Minimum 75% total allocation in equity & equity-related instruments. Mandatory minimum allocations:
     - Minimum **25% in Large Cap** companies.
     - Minimum **25% in Mid Cap** companies.
     - Minimum **25% in Small Cap** companies.

2. **Large Cap Fund**:
   - **Investment Objective**: Open-ended equity scheme predominantly investing in Large Cap companies.
   - **Asset Allocation Mandate**: Minimum **80% in equity shares of Large Cap stocks** (defined by AMFI as top 100 stocks by full market capitalization).

3. **Large & Mid Cap Fund**:
   - **Investment Objective**: Open-ended equity scheme investing in both Large Cap and Mid Cap stocks.
   - **Asset Allocation Mandate**: Minimum **35% in Large Cap stocks** AND minimum **35% in Mid Cap stocks**.

4. **Mid Cap Fund**:
   - **Investment Objective**: Open-ended equity scheme investing in Mid Cap stocks.
   - **Asset Allocation Mandate**: Minimum **65% in Mid Cap stocks** (defined by AMFI as stocks ranked 101st to 250th in market capitalization).

5. **Small Cap Fund**:
   - **Investment Objective**: Open-ended equity scheme investing in Small Cap stocks.
   - **Asset Allocation Mandate**: Minimum **65% in Small Cap stocks** (defined by AMFI as 251st company onwards by market capitalization). High risk and return volatility.

6. **Flexi Cap Fund**:
   - **Investment Objective**: Open-ended dynamic equity scheme investing across Large, Mid, and Small Cap stocks.
   - **Asset Allocation Mandate**: Minimum **65% in equity and equity-related instruments**. No restrictive sub-limits across market caps; fund managers have complete flexibility to shift allocation based on market valuation.

7. **ELSS (Equity Linked Savings Scheme)**:
   - **Investment Objective**: Open-ended equity scheme with tax benefits and statutory lock-in.
   - **Asset Allocation Mandate**: Minimum **80% in equity & equity-related securities**.
   - **Lock-in & Tax Features**: Mandatory statutory lock-in period of **3 years** from the date of unit allotment. Qualifies for tax deduction under Section 80C up to ₹1,50,000 per year under the Old Tax Regime.

8. **Sectoral / Thematic Fund**:
   - **Investment Objective**: Open-ended equity scheme concentrating in a specific industrial sector (e.g. Technology, Banking, Healthcare) or thematic trend (e.g. Infrastructure, ESG).
   - **Asset Allocation Mandate**: Minimum **80% in equity securities of the specified sector/theme**. High concentration risk. Portfolio overlap across different sectoral schemes of an AMC is capped at 50%.

9. **Focused Fund**:
   - **Investment Objective**: High-conviction equity scheme focused on a limited number of stocks.
   - **Asset Allocation Mandate**: Minimum **65% in equity**. Maximum number of stocks permitted in the portfolio is strictly restricted to **30 stocks**.

### 1.2 Debt Scheme Categories & Macaulay Duration Mandates

1. **Overnight Fund**:
   - **Maturity Limit**: Investments in overnight securities having maturity of **1 day** only (e.g. CBLO, Triparty Repo, Reverse Repo). Minimal credit and interest rate risk.

2. **Liquid Fund**:
   - **Maturity Limit**: Debt and money market securities with maturity **up to 91 days only**.
   - **Graded Exit Load**: Exit load applies if redeemed within 7 days of allotment (Day 1: 0.0070% down to Day 7: 0.0000%). Capital preservation and liquidity priority.

3. **Ultra Short Duration Fund**:
   - **Macaulay Duration**: Investments in debt & money market instruments such that the Macaulay Duration of the portfolio is between **3 months and 6 months**.

4. **Low Duration Fund**:
   - **Macaulay Duration**: Portfolio Macaulay Duration between **6 months and 12 months**.

5. **Money Market Fund**:
   - **Maturity Limit**: Investments in Money Market instruments having maturity up to **1 year** (e.g. Commercial Papers, Certificates of Deposit, Treasury Bills).

6. **Short Duration Fund**:
   - **Macaulay Duration**: Portfolio Macaulay Duration between **1 year and 3 years**. Suitable for conservative short-to-medium term horizons.

7. **Medium Duration Fund**:
   - **Macaulay Duration**: Portfolio Macaulay Duration between **3 years and 4 years**.

8. **Corporate Bond Fund**:
   - **Credit Allocation Mandate**: Minimum **80% investment in highest-rated corporate bonds** (AA+ and AAA rated debt paper). Moderate interest rate sensitivity, high credit safety.

9. **Credit Risk Fund**:
   - **Credit Allocation Mandate**: Minimum **65% investment in corporate bonds rated AA and below** (below highest rating). Higher credit default risk in exchange for potential yield enhancement.

10. **Gilt Fund**:
    - **Government Securities Mandate**: Minimum **80% investment in Government Securities (G-Secs)** across maturities. Zero credit default risk, but exposed to interest rate risk.

---

### 1.3 Hybrid Scheme Categories & Equity-Debt Limits

1. **Conservative Hybrid Fund**:
   - **Allocation Mandate**: **10% to 25% in equity** & equity-related instruments; **75% to 90% in debt securities**.

2. **Balanced Hybrid / Aggressive Hybrid Fund**:
   - **Balanced Hybrid**: 40% to 60% in equity; 40% to 60% in debt (no arbitrage permitted).
   - **Aggressive Hybrid**: **65% to 80% in equity**; **20% to 35% in debt securities**. Taxed as an equity-oriented fund.

3. **Dynamic Asset Allocation / Balanced Advantage Fund (BAF)**:
   - **Allocation Mandate**: Dynamic allocation between equity and debt from **0% to 100%** based on proprietary valuation models (P/E, P/B, interest rate indicators). Automated rebalancing without investor tax impact.

4. **Arbitrage Fund**:
   - **Allocation Mandate**: Minimum **65% in equity and equity-related instruments**, predominantly executing cash-futures market arbitrage. Low directional equity risk; treated as equity fund for tax purposes.

## SECTION 2: SEBI RISK-O-METER DISCLOSURE AND EVALUATION FRAMEWORK

The Risk-o-meter is an objective, mathematical risk disclosure tool mandatory across all mutual fund schemes.

### 2.1 Standardized Six-Level Risk Scale
The Risk-o-meter evaluates portfolio holdings every month and assigns one of six risk levels:
1. **Low**: Minimal principal risk; high liquidity, short duration (e.g. Overnight, Liquid funds).
2. **Low to Moderate**: Low interest rate sensitivity, high credit quality (e.g. Money Market, Ultra Short Duration funds).
3. **Moderate**: Balanced risk profile; moderate Macaulay duration or high-grade corporate bonds.
4. **Moderately High**: Moderate equity exposure or medium-duration debt funds (e.g. Conservative Hybrid, Arbitrage).
5. **High**: Significant equity allocation across Large/Mid Cap stocks or long-term Gilt funds.
6. **Very High**: High market volatility or sectoral concentration (e.g. Small Cap, Sectoral/Thematic funds).

### 2.2 Mathematical Risk Scoring Methodology
AMCs compute Risk-o-meter scores on a numerical scale of 1.0 to 6.0:

#### Debt Portfolio Risk Score Components
1. **Liquidity Risk Score**: Based on impact cost, issuer class, and listing status of debt instruments.
2. **Credit Risk Score**: Based on credit rating assigned by accredited rating agencies (AAA = 1 point, AA+ = 2 points, down to Unrated = 6 points).
3. **Interest Rate Risk Score**: Based on Macaulay Duration of the portfolio (Duration < 0.5 yr = 1 point, > 4.5 yrs = 6 points).

#### Equity Portfolio Risk Score Components
1. **Market Capitalization Score**: Large Cap = 5 points, Mid Cap = 7 points, Small Cap = 9 points.
2. **Volatility Score**: Based on annualized standard deviation of daily stock returns over 2 years.
3. **Impact Cost (Liquidity) Score**: Based on average impact cost for executing standard transaction sizes.

### 2.3 Reporting & Compliance Norms
- **Monthly Recalculation**: AMCs must evaluate the Risk-o-meter score as of the last day of each month.
- **Timeline**: Disclosed on AMC websites and AMFI portal within **10 days from the close of each month**.
- **Investor Communication**: Any change in Risk-o-meter level must be sent via email/SMS to unit holders, and highlighted in fund fact sheets for 12 months.
