# SEBI Mutual Fund Scheme Categorization & Risk-o-meter Guidelines

*Official Source:* Securities and Exchange Board of India (SEBI) & Association of Mutual Funds in India (AMFI) (`https://www.sebi.gov.in`)  
*Regulatory Framework:* SEBI Circulars on Categorization and Rationalization of Mutual Fund Schemes & Risk-o-meter Disclosure Norms  
*Trust Tier:* Tier 1 - Official Regulatory Body  

---

## 1. Scheme Categorization Framework

To harmonize mutual fund scheme definitions across Asset Management Companies (AMCs) and ensure true-to-label scheme names for retail investors, SEBI classifies mutual fund schemes into broad standardized categories.

### 1.1 Equity Scheme Categories
1. **Multi Cap Fund**: Minimum 75% investment in equity and equity-related instruments, with mandatory minimum allocation of 25% each in Large Cap, Mid Cap, and Small Cap stocks.
2. **Large Cap Fund**: Minimum 80% investment in equity shares of Large Cap companies (top 100 stocks by market capitalization).
3. **Large & Mid Cap Fund**: Minimum 35% in Large Cap and minimum 35% in Mid Cap companies.
4. **Mid Cap Fund**: Minimum 65% in Mid Cap companies (101st to 250th stocks by market capitalization).
5. **Small Cap Fund**: Minimum 65% in Small Cap companies (251st company onwards by market capitalization).
6. **Flexi Cap Fund**: Minimum 65% investment in equity instruments with dynamic allocation across Large, Mid, and Small Cap stocks without rigid cap restrictions.
7. **ELSS (Equity Linked Savings Scheme)**: Minimum 80% in equity, subject to a statutory lock-in period of 3 years. Qualifies for Section 80C tax deduction under Old Tax Regime.
8. **Sectoral / Thematic Fund**: Minimum 80% investment in a specific sector (e.g. Banking, Technology, Healthcare) or theme. Max portfolio overlap across sectoral schemes is capped at 50%.
9. **Focused Fund**: Portfolio focused on maximum 30 stocks with minimum 65% in equity.

### 1.2 Debt Scheme Categories
1. **Liquid Fund**: Investments in debt and money market instruments with maturity up to 91 days only. High liquidity, low credit and interest rate risk.
2. **Overnight Fund**: Investments in overnight securities having maturity of 1 day.
3. **Ultra Short Duration Fund**: Investments in debt instruments with Macaulay Duration between 3 months and 6 months.
4. **Short Duration Fund**: Investments in debt instruments with Macaulay Duration between 1 year and 3 years.
5. **Corporate Bond Fund**: Minimum 80% investment in highest-rated corporate bonds (AA+ and AAA rated paper).
6. **Gilt Fund**: Minimum 80% investment in Government Securities (G-Secs) across maturities. Zero credit default risk.

### 1.3 Hybrid Scheme Categories
1. **Conservative Hybrid Fund**: 10% to 25% in equity; 75% to 90% in debt instruments.
2. **Balanced Advantage / Dynamic Asset Allocation Fund**: Dynamic asset allocation between equity and debt instruments based on market valuation models without fixed limits.
3. **Aggressive Hybrid Fund**: 65% to 80% in equity; 20% to 35% in debt instruments.
4. **Arbitrage Fund**: Minimum 65% in equity arbitrage positions (simultaneous buying in cash market and selling in futures market). Taxed as equity fund.

## 2. Mutual Fund Risk-o-meter Framework

The Risk-o-meter is a mandatory visual risk indicator depicted on all scheme documents, fund fact sheets, and marketing literature to convey the portfolio risk profile to investors.

### 2.1 Standardized Risk Levels
The Risk-o-meter evaluates portfolio holdings monthly and assigns one of six standardized risk levels:
1. **Low**: Capital preservation focus; minimal market or credit volatility (e.g. Liquid, Overnight funds).
2. **Low to Moderate**: Moderate interest rate sensitivity with high credit safety (e.g. Ultra Short Duration funds).
3. **Moderate**: Balanced risk profile (e.g. Corporate Bond, Short Duration debt funds).
4. **Moderately High**: Moderate equity exposure or credit risk (e.g. Conservative Hybrid, Arbitrage funds).
5. **High**: Significant equity allocation across Large/Mid Cap stocks or long-duration G-Secs.
6. **Very High**: High volatility investments (e.g. Small Cap, Sectoral/Thematic equity funds, Micro-cap stocks).

### 2.2 Objective Risk Valuation Methodology
AMCs must compute Risk-o-meter scores on a scale of 1 to 6 using detailed mathematical criteria:
- **Debt Securities**: Evaluated on Liquidity Risk (impact cost), Credit Risk (agency rating score), and Interest Rate Risk (Macaulay Duration).
- **Equity Securities**: Evaluated on Market Capitalization (Large vs Mid vs Small), Volatility (daily standard deviation), and Impact Cost (liquidity score).
- **Mandatory Disclosures**: AMCs must disclose the Risk-o-meter status along with portfolio disclosures on their websites within 10 days from the end of each month. Any change in Risk-o-meter rating must be communicated directly to unit holders.
